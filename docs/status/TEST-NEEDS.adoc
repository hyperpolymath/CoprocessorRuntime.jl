= TEST-NEEDS: CoprocessorRuntime.jl

What tests exist for the Julia package, what CI runs, and what is missing.
Every count below comes from a run of `julia --project=. -e 'using Pkg; Pkg.test()'`
with the test sets set to verbose, on Julia 1.11.7 and 1.10.10, at commit
`6ccf6c9`. Counts are of assertions that ran, not of lines or functions.

== Grade

No CRG or TRG grade is established by this document. `status/READINESS.adoc`
carries `Current Grade: X` (not graded) and its per-component table is
unfilled. The test census below is evidence a grade assessment could use; it is
not an assessment, and it does not show coverage of the source.

== Package tests (`test/`)

`test/runtests.jl` runs seven test sets under one top-level `CoprocessorRuntime`
set.

[cols="3,1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Test set | Assertions | Source

| Aqua (`Aqua.test_all`) | 11 | `test/runtests.jl`
| smoke | 4 | `test/cases/smoke.jl`
| tropical grades | 10 | `test/cases/grades.jl`
| sessions and nullity | 10 | `test/cases/sessions.jl`
| epistemic receipts | 4 | `test/cases/receipts.jl`
| choreography and hub_ceiling | 7 | `test/cases/choreography.jl`
| AcceleratorGateExt (subprocess) | 1 | `test/runtests.jl` (runs `test/ext`)
|===

Result on Julia 1.11.7: 47 passed of 47. Result on Julia 1.10.10: 46 passed,
1 broken, of 47; the broken one is the `AcceleratorGateExt` entry, recorded as
`@test_skip` because `test/ext` needs Julia 1.11 (`[sources]`). Setting
`COPROCESSORRUNTIME_SKIP_EXT_TESTS=1` on 1.11 gives the same 46 passed and
1 broken.

=== Extension tests (`test/ext`)

On Julia 1.11 the subprocess runs `test/ext/runtests.jl` in its own environment
(AcceleratorGate fetched by URL at a pinned commit). It has five test sets and
24 assertions, 24 passed of 24: the extension is loaded (2); the table covers
every AcceleratorGate backend subtype (1); each of the 15 backends maps to its
kind (15); the four backends with no kind here map to `:unknown` (4); a mapped
kind opens a session (2).

== What CI runs

`.github/workflows/julia-ci.yml` has three jobs, read from the file; this
document does not report their results:

* Format: JuliaFormatter check over `src/` and `test/` including `test/cases` (not `ext/` or `test/ext`) (Julia `1`).
* Static analysis: `JET.test_package(CoprocessorRuntime)` (Julia `1`).
* Test: `Pkg.test()` (Test and Aqua) on Julia `1.10` and `1`, Ubuntu.

Other workflows exist under `.github/workflows/` (for example `estate-rules.yml`
runs the repository-shape, AsciiDoc and session-contract checks). The shell
tests under `tests/` check repository structure and estate tooling. They do not
test the Julia sources in `src/` directly, and they were not run for this document.

== What is not covered

Stated from reading `src/` against `test/`; not measured. No coverage tool has
been run, and the census above has no denominator of source lines or branches.

* No test of `EchoResidue` or `TransferMap` equality, nor of
  `injective_transfer` on its own (they appear only as inputs to other tests).
* No test of `kind_label` for each of the eight kinds, nor of the `UnknownKind`
  fallback method.
* No test of `observe_null` rejecting an invalid status, as far as the case
  files show.
* No property-based or fuzz tests. `tests/fuzz/README.adoc` says no harness is
  configured.
* No benchmark of the package. `benches/template_bench.sh` is inherited from
  the template and does not time Julia code.
* No formal proof. `verification/proofs/` holds inherited scaffolding; nothing
  there is a proof about this package, and CNO/OND remain claims
  (`:unproved` or `:model_checked`).
* No run on Julia 1.10 of the extension tests, by design of the `[sources]`
  requirement.
* The Format and JET jobs were not run for this document, so their current
  result is unknown here.
